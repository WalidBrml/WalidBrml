## 📊 Featured Projects

### 1. DZ Market Intelligence
**Business Intelligence & Demand Forecasting**

An end-to-end retail analytics platform designed to transform supermarket data into actionable business insights.

- Processed 468K+ sales lines and 4.5M+ inventory records.
- Analyzed 15 simulated stores and 500 products over three years.
- Developed SQL analytics pipelines and demand forecasting models.
- Built a six-page Streamlit application and Power BI dashboard.

**Technologies:** Python, SQL, Machine Learning, Power BI, Streamlit.

[![View Project](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-View_Project-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github)](https://github.com/WalidBrml/dz-market-intelligence)

### 2. Waxoria
**Intelligent Inventory & Demand Forecasting**

An intelligent inventory management and demand forecasting platform.

- Processed 913K daily sales records.
- Analyzed 500 store-product demand series.
- Implemented forecasting and inventory optimization.
- Random Forest reduced MAE by 1.72 units per series against the seven-day average baseline across 97.6% of series.

**Technologies:** Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Streamlit, Power BI.

[View Repository](https://github.com/WalidBrml/waxoria-forecasting)

### 3. E-Commerce Sales Analytics

An analytical project exploring Brazilian e-commerce data to understand sales performance and customer behavior.

- Analyzed approximately 100K orders from the Olist dataset.
- Developed 19 PostgreSQL analytical queries.
- Built a Power BI dashboard to monitor business KPIs.

**Technologies:** Python, PostgreSQL, Pandas, Power BI.

[View Repository](https://github.com/WalidBrml/ecommerce-sales-analytics)
